# 🚀 AstroVitals: Autonomous Bio-Digital Twin & AI Flight Surgeon
### NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 • Challenge #5: "Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions"

> **"When Houston is 20 Minutes Away, Your AI Flight Surgeon is 0 Seconds Away."**

AstroVitals is an autonomous, offline-first, AI-assisted crew health companion and bio-digital twin designed for long-duration deep space missions (Artemis Lunar Base & Mars Transit Alpha).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Interactive Holographic SVG Bio-Twin:**
   * Dynamic heartbeat pulse calibrated directly to calculated BPM (`animation-duration: 60/BPM`).
   * Clickable anatomical focus filters: Cardiovascular, Trabecular Skeletal Density, and Cranial/SANS fluid shift.
2. **Individual Baseline Drift Engine (Dual-Wave Telemetry):**
   * Benchmarks astronauts against their **individual pre-flight Earth baseline**, rather than invalid terrestrial norms.
3. **Simulated Mars Comm Blackout (Offline-First Autonomy):**
   * Solves deep-space 22.4-minute one-way communication delays with local deterministic clinical intervention.
4. **Actionable Countermeasures Grounded in NASA Data:**
   * Generates protocol-level prescriptions citing **NASA-STD-3001 Vol 1 Rev B**, **NASA HRP**, and **LSDA / OSDR**.
   * Interactive `[Execute Countermeasure Protocol]` button demonstrating vital stabilization in real-time.
5. **Mission Day Stress-Test Simulator (Day 1 to 500):**
   * Dynamic physiological model showing long-term trabecular bone loss, cardiovascular deconditioning, and circadian disruption.
